## Steam Workshop (BBCode)

```
[h1]Dragon's Lantern[/h1]
A belt lantern for The Witcher 3 Remastered, inspired by the Arisen's lantern in Dragon's Dogma. Hang it on Geralt's hip and keep both hands free for the sword.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Equip the Belt Lantern in a Pockets slot and it hangs from Geralt's left hip. Right hip, front-left and small of the back are options too.
[*]Auto light: lights when equipped and goes out when removed. Can also light only at night, or be manual only.
[*]Light or douse it by hand from the quick slot (middle mouse / R1 / RB). It never goes into his hand.
[*]Burns Lantern Oil and refills itself from flasks you carry.
[*]Swimming or diving puts the flame out.
[*]Swings as Geralt moves. Hidden in cutscenes and dialogue.
[*]Sold by innkeepers and shopkeepers.
[*]Settings under Options > Mods > Dragon's Lantern: position, brightness, light radius, oil use and more.
[*]Text in all 17 languages the game supports.
[/list]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
Buy a Belt Lantern and some Lantern Oil from any innkeeper or shopkeeper, then put the lantern in a Pockets slot.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered (5.0 or later), PC.

[h2]Settings menu (optional)[/h2]
The mod works out of the box with sensible defaults. To change position, brightness, oil use and more in Options > Mods > Dragon's Lantern, download the settings file and put it in [i]The Witcher 3\bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\[/i]:
[url=https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern/raw/main/src/menu/modDragonsLantern.xml]modDragonsLantern.xml[/url]

[h2]Bugs and feedback[/h2]
Please report bugs on GitHub. The form there asks for the details needed to fix them:
[url=https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern/issues]https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern/issues[/url]

The full source is on GitHub too, if you want to see how it's made:
[url=https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern]https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern[/url]

[i]A free fan mod, not affiliated with CD PROJEKT RED.[/i]
```

## Mod.io (Markdown)

# Dragon's Lantern

A belt lantern for The Witcher 3 Remastered, inspired by the Arisen's lantern in Dragon's Dogma. Hang it on Geralt's hip and keep both hands free for the sword.

## Features
- Equip the Belt Lantern in a Pockets slot and it hangs from Geralt's left hip. Right hip, front-left and small of the back are options too.
- Auto light: lights when equipped and goes out when removed. Can also light only at night, or be manual only.
- Light or douse it by hand from the quick slot. It never goes into his hand.
- Burns Lantern Oil and refills itself from flasks you carry.
- Swimming or diving puts the flame out.
- Swings as Geralt moves. Hidden in cutscenes and dialogue.
- Sold by innkeepers and shopkeepers.
- Settings under Options > Mods > Dragon's Lantern.
- Text in all 17 languages the game supports.

## Requirements
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered (5.0 or later), PC.

## Settings menu (optional)
Works out of the box with sensible defaults. For the Options > Mods > Dragon's Lantern page, download [modDragonsLantern.xml](https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern/raw/main/src/menu/modDragonsLantern.xml) and put it in `The Witcher 3\bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\`.

## Bugs and feedback
Please report bugs on GitHub: https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern/issues

Source code: https://github.com/remathes/DragonLantern

*A free fan mod, not affiliated with CD PROJEKT RED.*
